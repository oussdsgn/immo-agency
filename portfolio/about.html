About

I'm Benyahia Hicham, an art director and packaging designer who brings structural viability and production efficiency to commercial brands. I balance creative vision with industrial manufacturing constraints and turn product strategy into cohesive, shelf-ready packaging. I specialize in structural packaging and 3D product rendering, with a strong understanding of custom dielines, offset printing, flexography and foil finishing. Today I run Dar Art Pack, my studio for design, pre-press and visuals.
